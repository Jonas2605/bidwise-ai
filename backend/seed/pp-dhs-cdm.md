# Past Performance — DHS Continuous Diagnostics and Mitigation (CDM)

**Contract:** HSHQDC-18-D-00012  
**Customer:** U.S. Department of Homeland Security  
**Period of Performance:** 2019–2023  
**Value:** $4.8M (task order)

## Scope
Aether Federal provided 24x7 security operations support and endpoint telemetry integration for a civilian agency CDM task order. The team ingested logs from 14,000 endpoints into a Splunk-based SOC, tuned detection rules, and produced monthly ATO evidence packages.

## Results
- Reduced mean time to detect (MTTD) from 18 hours to 47 minutes within nine months.
- Closed 96% of Priority-1 findings before the quarterly Authority to Operate review.
- Delivered NIST 800-53 Rev. 5 control mappings for AU, SI, CM, and IR families.
- Maintained a 99.6% SLA for ticket response without a single Cure Notice.

## Relevance to future proposals
This effort demonstrates our ability to operate in a FISMA High environment, staff a cleared SOC, and produce CPARS-ready documentation. Key personnel (Maya Chen, SOC Lead) remain available for follow-on work.
